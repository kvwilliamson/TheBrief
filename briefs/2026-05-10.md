### Core Intelligence Claims

- AI agent effectiveness depends on building a 'scaffolding' comprising various components like skills, MCPs, and scripts.

- Plugins package entire workflows with app integrations, MCP servers, and hooks to enable easy installation and sharing.
- Hooks and scripts ensure deterministic actions within workflows, preventing reliance on a model's memory or judgment.
- Marketplaces are becoming the primary mechanism for distributing AI agent components, including plugins.

### Positioning Implication
